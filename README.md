# Networks Gross Margin Bridge

**Live page:** https://tran23qi.github.io/Networks-Margin-Bridge/

Sales grew 21%, but gross margin fell 2.4 points. This project splits that drop into product mix, price and cost effects, using a simulated telecom deal portfolio.

> All deal data is simulated and illustrative. It is not Ericsson data. Public Ericsson figures come from the Second quarter report 2026.

## Files
- `Ericsson_Networks_Margin_Bridge.xlsx`: 123 simulated deal lines, the margin bridge, a discount analysis by market area, and a waterfall chart. Change the two yellow cells on the Margin Bridge sheet to compare any two quarters.
- `Ericsson_Networks_Margin_Bridge.pptx`: three slides covering what moved, why it moved, and the recommendation.

## Findings
| Effect | Impact | Driver |
|---|---|---|
| Mix | −1.1 pp | Rollout services rose from 13% to 15% of sales, at a 15% margin |
| Price | −0.5 pp | Deeper hardware discounts in one market area; software discounts in EMEA |
| Cost | −0.7 pp | Radio hardware unit cost rose 2.7%, and prices did not follow |
